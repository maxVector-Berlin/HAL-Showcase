VOICE · AVATAR · EXPRESSION

Speech becomes a performance.

HAL does more than play an audio file. Spoken text is cleaned for pronunciation, synthesized with the active character's voice direction and paired with a timed viseme track. The avatar follows that track while separate expression logic adds attention, thought and humor.

  1. ListenPush-to-talk or typed input enters the same conversation.
  2. SpeakGerman or English TTS uses the selected HAL, Milo or Catbot profile.
  3. AnimatePhonemes map to visible mouth groups with pause-aware timing and soft transitions.
  4. PerformBlinks, smiles, thinking faces and idle behavior prevent a static portrait.

PROMPTS · COMFYUI · PHOTOSHOP

From rough idea to chosen result.

A request can become one direct render or a controlled selection process. HAL generates three distinct prompt options, waits for Guido to choose, then opens the model-specific setup instead of guessing the production settings.

  1. DescribeNatural German input becomes a production-ready English prompt.
  2. ChooseRender option 1, 2, 3, a subset or all three variants.
  3. ConfigureWorkflow, model, LoRA, strength, size, aspect ratio and seed.
  4. ContinueRepeat, revise or open chosen results directly in Photoshop.

CONTEXT · DOSSIERS · DESKTOP

Memory that appears when it matters.

Recent conversation keeps the current exchange coherent. Structured dossiers hold durable knowledge about people, projects, vehicles, places or devices. Trigger terms retrieve a concise summary first and deeper sections only when the topic needs them.

  1. MatchNames and related terms connect the message to the right dossier.
  2. RetrieveOnly relevant facts and open topics enter the active context.
  3. RespondHAL asks useful follow-ups grounded in what is already known.
  4. ActApproved actions launch programs or prepare a Photoshop document.

CAPITAL.COM DEMO · GOLD · OIL

Automation with visible guardrails.

The trading module observes commodities, evaluates entries and places DEMO orders through separately configured credentials. The broker account remains the source of truth: HAL reconciles positions before allowing new entries and records the broker's final booking value after closure.

  1. ObserveScan selected markets for confirmed pullbacks and local zones.
  2. ValidateCheck spread, direction, potential, stop risk, slots and daily limits.
  3. ProtectAttach stop, target and holding time; reserve each entry atomically.
  4. ReconcileCompare local state with the broker and import the final result.
